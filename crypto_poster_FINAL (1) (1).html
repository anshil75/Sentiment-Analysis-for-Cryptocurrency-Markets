Problem Statement

Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile, driven by public sentiment. This project applies machine learning to automatically classify crypto news and social media text as positive, neutral, or negative, then correlates daily sentiment with Bitcoin price movement across 6 years (2018–2023).

Preprocessing Pipeline
01Lowercase + noise removal — URLs, @mentions, #hashtags, emojis, special characters stripped
02Crypto slang normalisation — moon→surge increase, rekt→loss bankrupt, FUD→fear uncertainty doubt (domain adaptation)
03Stopword removal — English stopwords + crypto noise words (bitcoin, price, market — appear equally in all classes, zero signal)
04Lemmatization — crashing→crash, investors→investor, surging→surge (WordNet lemmatizer)
05TF-IDF Vectorization — 10,000 features, unigrams + bigrams, min_df=3, max_df=0.90, sublinear_tf=True. ~27 bot/price-ticker rows removed.

Conclusion

SVM (LinearSVC) achieved the best overall performance at 78.1% accuracy and F1 = 78.2%, closely followed by XGBoost at 78.0%. Linear models outperform non-linear models on TF-IDF feature spaces due to high-dimensional linear separability. Negative sentiment reliably identifies major market events — the FTX collapse and Luna crash are visible as sharp negative spikes in 2022. Sentiment-price correlation of r = 0.17 confirms a weak positive relationship consistent with published academic findings in financial NLP.
